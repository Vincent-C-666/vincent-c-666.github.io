EE Pocket 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-11-26

EE Pocket 是離線計算工具。所有計算都在你的裝置上完成，我們不使用廣告、分析或追蹤工具，也不需要帳號。

我們收集的資料

  • 問題回饋：只有在你主動使用「問題回饋／建議」頁面並按下送出時，才會傳送你填寫的內容、你選填的信箱，以及（可關閉的）App 版本與作業系統版本。這些資料透過 Formspree（formspree.io）轉寄給開發者，只用於回覆和改善 App，不會出售或分享給第三方。
  • 購買：Pro 解鎖由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資訊。
  • 本機設定：收藏、最近使用、上次輸入的數值和語言設定只存在你的裝置上。

刪除資料

若要刪除你送出的回饋，請再透過回饋頁面告知，我們會在 30 天內刪除。刪除 App 即會清除所有本機資料。

EE Pocket Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

EE Pocket is an offline calculator. All calculations run on your device. There are no ads, analytics or trackers, and no account is required.

Data we collect

  • Feedback: only when you open the Feedback page and tap Send, we transmit what you wrote, your optional e-mail address, and (optionally) the app and OS version. It is delivered to the developer through Formspree (formspree.io), used only to reply and improve the app, and never sold or shared.
  • Purchases: the Pro unlock is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Local settings: favorites, recents, last inputs and language stay on your device.

Deleting data

To have feedback you sent deleted, ask through the Feedback page and we will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
